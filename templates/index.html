Ways to Reduce Your Carbon Footprint

Six practical changes you can make today to lower your monthly CO₂ emissions.

Switch to Public Transport or Cycling

Replacing a weekly car commute with a bus or bike can cut transport emissions by up to 70%. Even one car-free day per week makes a measurable difference.

Up to 70% transport saving

Use Renewable Energy at Home

Switching to a green electricity tariff or installing solar panels can dramatically reduce your monthly electricity carbon impact without changing your lifestyle.

100% clean energy possible

Reduce Meat Consumption

Meat production — especially beef — is one of the highest-emission food categories. Cutting even 2 meat meals per week can save 50–100 kg CO₂ per year.

50–100 kg CO₂ saved / year

Fly Less, Choose Direct Flights

Aviation is one of the fastest ways to increase your footprint. Replacing one long-haul flight with a train journey or video call can save 500–2000 kg CO₂.

500–2000 kg CO₂ per flight

Reduce, Reuse & Recycle Waste

Landfill waste generates methane — a potent greenhouse gas. Composting food scraps, recycling packaging, and buying less disposable plastic all reduce your waste footprint.

Cuts methane emissions

Make Your Home More Energy Efficient

Improving insulation, switching to LED lighting, and upgrading to energy-efficient appliances can reduce home energy use by 20–40% with no lifestyle sacrifice.

20–40% energy reduction
